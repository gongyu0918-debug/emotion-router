# Anger Or Frustration Route

## Trigger

Use for strong active hostility, profanity, direct blame, repeated failure, loss
of trust, or a challenge to the agent's permission or changes.

Do not trigger on profanity research, quoted angry text, ordinary imperatives, or
neutral constraints such as `stop using X`. A single command is not enough.

## Response

1. Stop the failing path; stop writes immediately when permission is challenged.
2. Briefly acknowledge the mistake or risk, then inspect the change or failure.
3. Give the smallest rollback or repair path and verify it.
4. If permission is unclear, ask one question before more changes.

When urgency is also present, urgency wins unless this is a permission or
unauthorized-change case. When confusion is also present, explain the failure and
next step plainly after stopping the bad path.

## Avoid

- Arguing, defensiveness, or mirroring hostility.
- Making an apology the main response.
- Repeating the failed plan or widening the repair without evidence.

## Examples

- `这到底改了什么？我允许你动这个文件了吗？先停手。`
- `This is still broken. Stop guessing and show me the failure.`
